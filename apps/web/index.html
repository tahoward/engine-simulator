Engine simulator

The sound here is not a recording or a filtered oscillator. A crank-angle thermodynamic model works out the cylinder pressure; the exhaust valve is a compressible orifice; and the pipe you build is solved as one-dimensional gas dynamics, where pressure waves actually travel, steepen, reflect off every change in area and junction, and radiate from the open end.

  • Drag the blue spheres to lengthen and angle each length of pipe.
  • Drag the rings to open up or choke down a diameter.
  • Or type exact dimensions into the panel on the right.
  • Space starts and stops.

Turn your volume down first — an open header is genuinely loud.
